# Human Review Packet

## 1. Original goal

State what the task was supposed to achieve.

## 2. Actual change

Explain what actually changed in human-native language.

## 3. How human can check

List concrete human check steps.

## 4. Verified scenarios

List what was verified.

## 5. Unverified scenarios

List what was not verified.

## 6. Affected areas

List areas that may be affected.

## 7. Red flags

List warning signs human should watch for.

## 8. Risk explanation

Explain risk in human terms.

## 9. Recommendation

State close, reject, or escalate recommendation.

## 10. Human decision

Human final decision is recorded here.
