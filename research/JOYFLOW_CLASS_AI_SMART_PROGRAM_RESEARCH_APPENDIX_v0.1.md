## 1. General smart-program model

Current research does not support `AI everywhere`. The stronger candidate is:

```text
native / typed current facts
        ↓
mechanical sufficiency check
        ↓
Strong DIRECT where sufficient
        ↓ otherwise
bounded Class-AI on the minimum currently-material cognitive closure
        ↓
Full Brain only for genuinely open/new semantic frontier
        ↓
candidate
        ↓
independent/native/product-relative verification and effect gates
```

Class-AI does not own Truth, Authority, Authorization, currentness, atomicity, idempotency, commit legality or effect truth.

## 2. Class-AI scope

The number and range of Class-AI scopes are not fixed by App, Module, Node or Department count.

Current candidate rule:

`scope = minimum sufficient material decision closure + bounded optional frontier`.

A simple program may naturally converge to one logical Class-AI because its whole product is one small closure. A large system may expose several local temporary scopes. Logical scope count does not imply an equal number of physical models.

Scope may expand, shrink, split, temporarily merge, disappear into deterministic execution, or escalate to Full Brain. The scope selector cannot establish its own completeness; insufficient scope must trigger targeted reacquire/reopen or UNKNOWN/fail-closed behavior.

## 3. Split / merge

Do not merge two Class-AI scopes just because a cross-scope relation exists.

Preferred order:

1. preserve local closures;
2. exchange minimum verified typed outputs;
3. attempt deterministic composition;
4. expose the smallest missing typed distinction if needed;
5. form a temporary joint Class-AI closure only when correct candidate choice still requires joint semantic co-visibility;
6. use Full Brain when the relation is genuinely open/new rather than bounded.

Recurring joint cognition may later discover a stable typed interface and be replaced by deterministic composition.

## 4. Migration and lifecycle

Short product lifetime alone does not imply DIRECT. A short-lived product with frequent API/schema/requirement changes may benefit strongly from Class-AI because migration/adaptation work dominates.

Class-AI may help locate affected topology, preserve unaffected deterministic paths, generate adapters/mappings, temporarily cover reopened scope and propose successor deterministic forms.

Missing native evidence remains UNKNOWN; AI cannot create migration truth from absent evidence.

## 5. Learning / hardening distinction

A mastered cognitive capability does not have to become Strong DIRECT.

Hardening has three separate questions:

- validity: current Product requirements and product-relative safety are satisfied;
- desirability: it is worth doing given reuse, latency, cost, remaining lifetime, change rate, maintenance, IP/offline needs, etc.;
- effect: the durable implementation change actually passes the applicable engineering/version/authorization lifecycle.

Positive ROI is not a universal validity requirement. It is an optimization input unless Product requirements make it mandatory.

## 6. Security direction

For commercial software, current research favors keeping crown-jewel Class-AI/hardening capability server-side when practical and using a bounded Local-Lite fallback plus deterministic safety kernel/Strong DIRECT on the client.

Local-Lite should be capability-reduced: known domains, typed facts, bounded legal actions, no promotion authority and no new semantic/Authority role.

## 7. Joyflow-specific mapping

Joyflow keeps one Web Brain as the semantic/product reasoning layer, Local Codex as bounded executor, Machine/native evidence for mechanical truth/currentness, and user approval/acceptance/merge boundaries.

Class-AI may assist only after product/design meaning is frozen, especially for:

- contract/projection compilation assistance;
- affected-scope reconstruction;
- repair localization;
- migration/version candidate generation;
- typed-interface candidate generation;
- recurring-pattern deterministic hardening candidates.

Class-AI is not a second semantic Brain and does not create Product meaning, mutation authorization, acceptance, merge authority or native currentness.

## 8. Existing-expressibility discipline

Before adding any Joyflow Class-AI runtime/compiler component, replay the exact current Joyflow mechanisms first. Prefer:

`ALREADY_EXPRESSIBLE -> COMPOSE/NARROW_CONFIGURATION -> NARROW_EXTENSION only for demonstrated mechanical carrier gap`.

No current evidence requires a Global Class-AI Manager, a new approval plane, a second semantic Brain or a universal new lifecycle.
